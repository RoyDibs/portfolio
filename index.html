Stabilizing the European power grid after a fault

A 900 MW generation loss in Greece sends a frequency-deviation wave across the continent. Differentiable predictive control drives every node back to nominal in real time.

\( M_i\dot{\omega}_i = P_i - D_i\omega_i - \sum_j B_{ij}\sin(\delta_i-\delta_j) \)

CINOC — cardinality-invariant operator policies

Neural-operator policies for multi-agent control of incompressible flow, invariant to the number of actuators and sensors. The pipeline behind 1st place at the global Tesseract hackathon.

\( \partial_t \omega + (\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\omega = \nu\nabla^2\omega \)

Learning to control PDEs with time-integrated operators

Differentiable predictive control on a time-integrated DeepONet tracks a target profile with distributed actuators. The surrogate matches the solver to a median relative \(L_2\) error of 1.11%.

\( \partial_t u = D\,\partial_{xx} u + r\,u(1-u) \)

Learning hidden physics and system parameters

Deep operator networks that recover governing parameters from sparse, noisy data to 95% accuracy, extending earlier interface-aware PINNs for heterogeneous media.

\( \hat a \leftarrow \hat a - \gamma\,\nabla_a \lVert b - F(\hat a)\rVert^2 \)

Geometry-aware operators for moving interfaces

Dendritic solidification and other moving-boundary problems, together with agentic workflows for materials science, from my 2026 internship at the LLNL Data Science Institute.

\( \partial_t \phi = -M\,\frac{\delta \mathcal{F}}{\delta \phi} \)

Hybrid neural-operator + FEM fracture simulation

Speeds up crack-propagation simulation without losing accuracy, with a reinforcement-learning agent that steers the crack. Built with FEniCSx.
